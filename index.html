Mirrorfold

Twenty endless fractal worlds to fly through. They breathe, recolour and pulse with the music, and you can never hit a wall. Turn on Journey to sit back and be flown.

Each world hides a glowing orb. Fly into it. The first six you find each unlock a new palette. The Orbs button hides them.

For Apple Music or any other app: enter silent, start your song there, then set Music to Tap tempo and tap along.

Warning: photosensitivity and motion. This shows constantly changing colour, patterns and motion. A small number of people have seizures or feel unwell when they see visuals like these, even with no history of epilepsy. Do not use it if you have epilepsy or are sensitive to flashing light or motion. Stop at once if you feel dizzy, sick or unwell. You use it at your own risk.

Gallery

Journeys and packs that people have shared. They are kept with this site, and any picture programs in them have been looked at by a maintainer.

Offer to the gallery

Tick what you want to offer. What you added from the gallery is someone else's, so it is not listed. If a journey or the scene uses a world, palette, motion, filter or overlay that is listed here, that goes with it.

Offering opens a form on GitHub with only the ticked things in it. A check runs on GitHub, and a maintainer looks at the pack before it appears here.

Library

A pack is a small file of worlds, palettes, motions, filters, overlays, journey templates and sets. Nothing in a pack can reach the page or your data. Worlds, filters and overlays may hold a short picture program, which is checked before use. What you add is saved in this browser only.

New starts from a copy of what is showing now, and opens controls so you can change it while you watch. Each has a Help button.

Or move a pack as text: copy yours out of this box, or paste one in and load it.

This palette was not made from two colours, so these controls show the nearest match. Moving one rebuilds the palette from them.
